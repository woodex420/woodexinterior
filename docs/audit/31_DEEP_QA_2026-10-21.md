# Deep QA — Woodex OS, 21 October 2026

**Scope:** every screen in the admin (45 routes), the four business chains (enquiry → estimate → quotation → invoice → payment → project), the automation scheduler, the website studio/builder, and the repository itself.

**Verdict in one line:** the engine is real and wired; the *paths between screens* were not. An enquiry could become a lead but never an estimate, the browser tab said "Woodex OS" on eight screens, demo rows had no marker, and the sidebar advertised shipped features as future phases. Those are fixed in Slice A. The remaining gaps are listed below in the order I recommend fixing them.

### Slice E — Invoice automation that actually runs — DONE (26 Oct)
**What was wrong.** The only invoice the OS raised by itself was the advance, at acceptance. Every later milestone
needed somebody to remember, so a job that was 70% built could still have one invoice raised. And there was no overdue
ladder at all: chasing money meant opening each invoice and pressing "remind" by hand. The Automation screen said
nothing about billing.

**What shipped.**
| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Sales\Actions\RaiseMilestoneInvoice` | one raiser for any project milestone: same number series, tax slice from the quotation, frozen PDF/snapshot, ledger posting and sequence trigger as the advance; a milestone is billed once, ever |
| `Sales\Actions\RaiseAdvanceInvoice` | now only the acceptance rules (accepted quotation → the `on_acceptance` milestone) and delegates the raising |
| `woodex:invoice-milestones` | hourly at :15 — bills every milestone an accepted quotation has reached, and picks up anything missed (a project accepted before this slice, a failed acceptance job); `--dry-run` |
| `Sales\Actions\InvoiceReminderLadder` + `woodex:invoice-reminders` | daily 10:00 — the 7/14/30-day ladder, one rung per invoice forever, read back from the audit trail so no new table is needed |
| `Sales\Notifications\OverdueInvoiceNotification` | bell + Telegram to whoever may verify payments: the rung, the days late, and — when the client was not messaged — exactly why |
| Automation catalog | both jobs listed with their cadence and on/off switches; pausing the ladder stops every client message at once |
| Settings | `invoice_reminder_rungs` (7/14/30) and `invoice_reminder_send_client` |

**Consent, unchanged.** The client is messaged only through the existing consent-gated `SendPaymentReminder` — no
opt-in, no phone number or no WhatsApp connection means no message, and the rung is still recorded with the reason so
the next step is a phone call rather than a silent failure. The ladder sends as the person who raised the invoice,
else the first active person who may verify payments, because the conversation action authorises a real staff member.

**Acceptance met.** An invoice 8 days past due produced exactly one 7-day reminder — nothing on a second run, nothing
the next day. 15 days late produced the 14-day rung, 31 days the 30-day rung: three rungs, three records, one client
message each. Paid, void and draft invoices never enter the ladder, and nothing fires before the due date.

**Live proof on 33143.** `schedule:list` shows `woodex:invoice-milestones` (hourly at :15) and
`woodex:invoice-reminders` (daily 10:00). `/admin/automation` lists **Milestone invoices** and **Overdue payment
ladder** with their cadence. A QA invoice (INV-QA-0001, Rs 50,000, 8 days late) fired exactly one rung: the audit
entry reads *"7-day reminder for INV-QA-0001 (8 days overdue): client not messaged — WhatsApp is not connected in
Settings yet."*, and a second run fired **0**. The QA invoice and its audit rows were deleted afterwards.

**Recurring invoices:** approved by the owner on 26 Oct and shipped the same day — see **Slice E2** below.

**Tests.** 10 in `tests/Feature/Sales/InvoiceAutomationTest.php`: milestone billed once, unreached left alone,
dry-run writes nothing, one rung per day-period, rungs advance with age, no consent → recorded with the reason,
paid/void/draft/not-due skipped, the Automation screen lists both, and the switch is honoured.

### Slice E2 — Recurring invoices, a retainer that bills itself — DONE (26 Oct)
**What was wrong.** Slice E could raise every project milestone and chase every overdue invoice, but the most common
studio retainer still needed a person: a monthly maintenance client, a quarterly inspection. Whoever remembered raised
the invoice; when nobody remembered, a month of work went unbilled. The owner approved **one new table** on 26 Oct to
close it.

**What shipped.**
| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| `recurring_invoices` table (+`recurring.invoice` sequence) | the schedule: client, title, business line, frequency, standing lines, tax, due days, start, next run, end, pause, run count, last run |
| `Sales\Enums\RecurringFrequency` | monthly / quarterly / yearly advancing with `addMonthNoOverflow` and friends — the 31st never silently becomes the 3rd |
| `Sales\Models\RecurringInvoice` | `scopeDue()`, money accessors from the standing lines, `label()` for the audit trail |
| `Sales\Services\InvoiceFreezer` | the freeze/snapshot step shared with `RaiseMilestoneInvoice`: idempotent, verifies the sha256, refuses a tampered document |
| `Sales\Actions\IssueRecurringInvoice` | bills one period: `kind = recurring`, `RI-2026-0001`, due date, items, frozen PDF, ledger posting, then advances the schedule |
| `woodex:invoice-recurring` | daily 09:20, `--dry-run`, listed on `/admin/automation` as **Recurring invoices** and toggleable |
| `/admin/invoices?tab=recurring` | list with the standing total and next run; create, pause/resume, **Bill now**, delete |

**One invoice, not a backlog.** A schedule that missed three periods — server down, a client skip, a forgotten
deploy — raises **one** invoice for today and walks `next_issue_on` forward past today. Catch-up debt is a billing
decision for the owner, not something a cron should invent.

**Acceptance met.** One invoice per period with its own `RI-` series, 15% tax on Rs 25,000 → Rs 28,750, due +7 days

schedule; the `sales` role is refused.

**Tests.** 9 in `tests/Feature/Sales/RecurringInvoicesTest.php` (listed above in Step 31).
Full suite **1,341 passed · 1 skipped · 7,210 assertions**; Pint clean; PHPStan 0 (598 files).
