The thinking behind the space

A thoughtful
starting point.

An open-plan home needs to feel connected without losing its sense of place. This concept uses carefully defined zones to give dining, living, and circulation their own identity.

The approach

Timber screens soften transitions without shutting out natural light. A restrained mix of black detailing, stone surfaces, and warm timber brings visual order to the open layout.

The finer details

Furniture placement becomes an architectural tool. Long horizontal lines establish calm, while the stair and vertical timber elements introduce contrast and movement.

Sample concept, not a completed client commission. Gallery images illustrate the design direction and may depict different properties.
