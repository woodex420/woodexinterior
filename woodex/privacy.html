Privacy, simply put.

How this starter website handles information.

This is a starter website

Woodex Interior’s business details and portfolio are sample content. This page describes the functionality of this supplied static theme, not a verified business privacy policy. The website owner must review and adapt it before launch.

Enquiry information

In the default demo mode, the enquiry form prepares a downloadable text brief in your browser. Your name, email, phone, location, and project information are not transmitted to a server or saved by this website. Information remains in the form until you clear it, reset the page, or leave. If you download the brief, it is saved on your device.

Cookies and analytics

This theme does not set cookies, use local storage, or include third-party analytics. Fonts and images are stored locally. Your hosting provider may maintain standard server access logs; consult its policy for details.

Before enabling live enquiries

The website owner should identify the business operating this site, supply a privacy contact, and explain which form provider receives submissions. The final policy should also describe the purpose of processing, applicable legal basis, retention period, security measures, any international transfers, and the rights available to visitors under applicable law.
