Good to know

A little clarity,
before we begin.

How does a project begin?

Start by sharing your space, priorities, and any existing plans or inspiration. The first conversation helps define the scope and the level of design support you need.

Can you help with just one room?

The starter service offering includes both individual rooms and larger interior projects. A focused room scheme can include layout, colors, finishes, furniture, and styling.

How are design fees and timelines agreed?

They depend on the scope, size, and complexity of the project. A tailored proposal should set out the deliverables, design stages, fees, and schedule before work begins.

Do you design custom furniture and woodwork?

Bespoke design can include storage, cabinetry, wardrobes, and furniture concepts. Fabrication, installation, and specialist engineering should be confirmed separately in the project proposal.

What should I prepare for an initial conversation?

A few photographs, available measurements or plans, an approximate budget, and a short list of priorities are a helpful place to start. Inspiration images are welcome, too.
