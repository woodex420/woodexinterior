Clear answers before the work begins

What services does Woodex Interior provide? +

Interior design, fit-out, renovation, architecture, 3D visualization and custom furniture, all coordinated by one team from our Lahore studio.

Can Woodex manage a project from design to handover? +

Yes. Appoint us for one service, or let one accountable team handle design, quantities, site work, furniture and the final handover.

Does Woodex only work in Lahore? +

Lahore is our base. We also deliver selected projects in Islamabad, Karachi and other cities across Pakistan.

How is the cost of a project worked out? +

After a site visit we prepare an itemised quotation based on the scope, area, materials and site condition, so you know what each part costs before work starts.

Will I see the design before work begins? +

Yes. Layouts, materials and lighting are shown in photoreal 3D, and we revise them with you until the design is approved.

How do we begin a project? +

Share the location, approximate area, intended use, timeline and any drawings you already have. We will confirm the right next step within one working day.
